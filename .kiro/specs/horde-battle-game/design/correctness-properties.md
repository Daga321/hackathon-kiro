# Design: Correctness Properties

> **Related:** [Tasks](../tasks/gameplay-core.md) | [Tasks](../tasks/backend-auth.md)

---

*A property is a characteristic or behavior that must hold true across all valid executions of the system. Properties serve as a bridge between human-readable specifications and machine-verifiable correctness guarantees.*

---

### Property 1: Player and Enemy always remain within Arena bounds

*For any* Player or Enemy position and any movement vector or applied force, after processing the movement, the resulting position SHALL be within the Arena bounds (x ∈ [0, arenaWidth], y ∈ [0, arenaHeight]).

This property covers Player clamping (Req 1.2), Enemy direction recalculation at boundary (Req 3.3), and enemy separation force clamping (Req 3.4).

**Validates: Requirements 1.2, 3.3, 3.4**

---

### Property 2: Movement speed is always exactly 200 px/s for the Guard

*For any* valid combination of directional inputs (including diagonals), the magnitude of the Graveyard_Guard's velocity vector SHALL be exactly 200 px/s (with tolerance of ε = 0.001 px/s for floating-point arithmetic).

**Validates: Requirements 1.1, 1.5**

---

### Property 3: Guard melee range always exceeds undead melee range

*For any* valid game configuration (including post-MVP undead types), the value of `PLAYER_MELEE_RANGE` (64 px) SHALL be strictly greater than `ENEMY_MELEE_RANGE` (48 px) for all Undead types defined in UndeadConfig.

**Validates: Requirements 2.3**

---

### Property 4: Guard melee attack applies exactly 30 points to each undead in range

*For any* set of N Undead (N ≥ 1) within PLAYER_MELEE_RANGE at the moment of attack, the Combat_System SHALL apply exactly 30 damage points to each of them — no more, no less — regardless of their current HP.

**Validates: Requirements 2.5**

---

### Property 5: Attack cooldown guarantees at most 1 melee attack every 600 ms

*For any* sequence of attack inputs, the CombatSystem SHALL guarantee that no two Melee_Attacks are processed with less than 600 ms between them; inputs during cooldown are silently discarded.

**Validates: Requirements 2.7**

---

### Property 6: A melee attack with no enemies in range causes no damage effects

*For any* game state where no Undead is within PLAYER_MELEE_RANGE when the Graveyard_Guard attacks, the Combat_System SHALL execute the swing animation but SHALL NOT apply damage to any game object.

**Validates: Requirements 2.6**

---

### Property 7: Separation force between enemies eliminates overlaps without pushing them outside the Arena

*For any* cluster of N Enemies (N ≥ 2) whose centers are within 32 px of each other, after applying the separation force: (a) no pair of Enemies will have their centers within 32 px, and (b) no Enemy will be outside the Arena bounds.

**Validates: Requirements 3.4**

---

### Property 8: Base speed of standard Enemies is always 80 px/s

*For any* standard Enemy and any Player position within the Arena, the magnitude of the Enemy's velocity vector SHALL be exactly 80 px/s while not in collision separation state.

**Validates: Requirements 3.2**

---

### Property 9: Player HP never falls below 0

*For any* sequence of damage events applied to the Player (from any source, with any magnitude), the Player's HP SHALL remain in the range [0, 100] at all times. HP cannot be negative.

**Validates: Requirements 5.2**

---

### Property 10: An Enemy's HP bar reflects the hp/maxHp ratio accurately

*For any* Enemy with current HP in (0, maxHp), the visual width of the HealthBar SHALL be proportional to `hp / maxHp` with a tolerance of ε = 0.01 (1%).

**Validates: Requirements 4.3**

---

### Property 11: Killing an Enemy always adds exactly 10 points to the score

*For any* Enemy (regardless of how much overkill damage is dealt), when its HP reaches 0 or below, the Player's score SHALL increase by exactly 10 points — no more, no less.

**Validates: Requirements 4.5**

---

### Property 12: The enemy count formula per round is exactly 5 + (round − 1) × 3

*For any* round number r ∈ [1, 50], the WaveManager SHALL compute `enemyCountForRound(r) = 5 + (r - 1) * 3`, producing a positive integer. For r=1: 5; for r=50: 152.

**Validates: Requirements 6.1, 6.4, 6.7**

---

### Property 13: Enemy spawn positions respect the minimum distance from the Player

*For any* Player position within the Arena and any round number, all spawn positions generated by the WaveManager SHALL be: (a) within the 32 px interior strip of the Arena boundary, and (b) at a distance ≥ 100 px from the Player's center.

**Validates: Requirements 6.2**

---

### Property 14: The game-over screen displays exactly the finished session data

*For any* session state (round reached r, enemies killed k, score s), when game-over is triggered, the overlay SHALL display exactly the values r, k, and s — without truncating, rounding, or altering any of them.

**Validates: Requirements 8.2**

---

### Property 15: "Play Again" resets the game state to exact initial values

*For any* in-progress game state (arbitrary HP, arbitrary round, n live enemies), upon activating "Play Again", the system SHALL produce a new state with: HP = 100, round = 1, enemies = [] (empty), score = 0.

**Validates: Requirements 8.5**

---

### Property 16: Password validation accepts exactly lengths between 8 and 72 characters

*For any* candidate password string, the Auth_Service SHALL accept it if and only if `length ∈ [8, 72]` (inclusive). Any length outside this range SHALL be rejected with an error identifying the `password` field.

**Validates: Requirements 9.1, 9.3**

---

### Property 17: Registration retry logic respects the 3-attempt limit

*For any* number n of consecutive DynamoDB failures: if n ≤ 3, the Auth_Service SHALL perform exactly n retries and eventually return a valid token; if n > 3, SHALL mark the profile as `permanently_failed` and perform no further retries.

**Validates: Requirements 9.8**

---

### Property 18: The leaderboard is sorted by (highestRound DESC, totalScore DESC) in all contexts

*For any* set of user entries (global or friends), the service SHALL return the list sorted such that for every pair of consecutive entries (i, i+1): `entry[i].highestRound ≥ entry[i+1].highestRound`; and if equal in round: `entry[i].totalScore ≥ entry[i+1].totalScore`.

**Validates: Requirements 10.1, 11.5**

---

### Property 19: The global leaderboard returns at most 100 entries

*For any* number N of registered users (N can be any positive integer), the response from `GET /leaderboard/global` SHALL contain at most 100 entries.

**Validates: Requirements 10.2**

---

### Property 20: Each leaderboard entry contains the four required fields

*For any* leaderboard entry (global or friends), the returned object SHALL contain all fields: `rank`, `username`, `highestRound`, and `totalScore` — none can be null or absent.

**Validates: Requirements 10.4, 11.4, 11.6**

---

### Property 21: Protected endpoints reject any invalid token with HTTP 401

*For any* request to a protected endpoint with an invalid token (absent, expired, malformed, or from a revoked user), the system SHALL return exactly HTTP 401 — without processing the request or revealing data.

**Validates: Requirements 10.6, 16.1**

---

### Property 22: Deleting a friendship removes it from both lists immediately

*For any* pair of users A and B with a confirmed friendship, when A deletes the friendship: neither A appears in B's friend list, nor B appears in A's friend list, immediately after the operation.

**Validates: Requirements 11.7**

---

### Property 23: Anti-cheat rejects impossible rounds given the session duration

*For any* pair (sessionDuration, claimedRound), the Leaderboard_Service SHALL reject the submission when `claimedRound > MAX_PLAUSIBLE_ROUND(sessionDuration)`, where `MAX_PLAUSIBLE_ROUND(d) = floor(d / MIN_ROUND_DURATION_SECONDS)`.

**Validates: Requirements 16.3**

---

### Property 24: The HUD displays health in red if and only if HP < 30% of maximum

*For any* Player HP value: if `hp < 0.30 * maxHp`, the indicator SHALL render in red; if `hp ≥ 0.30 * maxHp`, SHALL render in the default color. The transition is immediate and bidirectional.

**Validates: Requirements 7.3, 7.4**
