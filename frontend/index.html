Game Guide

Horde System

Enemies attack in waves. Each wave brings more and stronger enemies. Survive as long as you can.

Enemy Aggro

Each wave has an aggro timer. When it reaches zero, all enemies become aggressive and rush towards you regardless of distance.

Enemy Types

Skeletons — Melee fighters, some carry swords for extra damage.
Slimes — Smaller and faster, harder to hit.

Health Pickups

Defeated enemies may drop hearts. Collect them to restore health. They blink before disappearing. Green dots on the minimap show their location.

Damage Indicators

White numbers = damage you deal.
Red numbers = damage you receive.
Green numbers = health restored.

Abilities

Attack — Strike nearby enemies. Hitting an enemy knocks it back and prevents it from attacking briefly.

Survival Tips

• Keep moving to avoid getting surrounded.
• Attack first — enemies can't hit you during knockback.
• Watch the minimap for enemy positions and health pickups.
• Pay attention to the aggro timer in the HUD.
